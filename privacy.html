AyurSathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AyurSathi is a bilingual (Hindi and English) wellness application that provides educational information about Ayurvedic daily routines, yoga mudras, herbal plants, healthy recipes, Unani terms and other wellness topics.

1. Information We Collect

AyurSathi is designed to work primarily offline. Wellness journal entries and other information you enter may be stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own user account system or require you to create an account.

The app may use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Advertising services may collect device identifiers, usage data or other information as described in Google's own privacy policies and settings. The information processed depends on the ad services and configuration used by the app.

2. How Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used to provide app features, such as your wellness journal and locally scheduled reminders. Advertising data may be used by the advertising provider to deliver, measure or improve advertisements, subject to its policies.

3. Local Storage and Notifications

Some information and reminder settings may remain on your device. Notifications may require permission from your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by third-party services used by the app, including Google Mobile Ads, according to their policies. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third parties.

5. Data Security and Retention

Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device. We do not maintain a separate AyurSathi user account database. Third-party services may retain information under their own retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

AyurSathi is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

7. Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. You may also manage advertising personalization through the available Google and device settings. Removing locally stored app data may delete your journal and preferences.

8. Health Disclaimer

AyurSathi provides general educational wellness information only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
